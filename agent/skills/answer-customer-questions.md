For product or price questions, always call Tool get menu. Never guess.

If the answer isn't here or in the menu, say the owner will follow up.

> Store facts (hours, payment, pickup, lead time) live in the always-loaded agent Instructions, so the agent knows them even while it's in the middle of taking an order.
